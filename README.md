# Medieval Island (Solarpunk Farm)

A full-featured 3D medieval island farming and clean arcane magic simulation built in React, Three.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.

## Features

- **3D Interactive Realm (`Realm`)**:
  - Real-time 3D island spanning Green Valley, Deep Forest, and Southern Wetlands.
  - 24-hour day/night celestial phases (Sunrise, Zenith, Sunset, Night) with dynamic shadows, ambient brightness, and starlit skies.
  - Weather simulation (Clear, Cloudy, Rainy, Fierce Storm, Heatwave, Gale, Misty Fog) dynamically affecting solar, wind, and crop growth.
  - Third-person player avatar with WASD keyboard movement, virtual touch joystick, sprint, camera orbit and zoom.
  - Proximity interaction badges for cultivating fields, petting animals, drawing sacred water, cooking at the campfire, fishing, and speaking with citizens.

- **Sacred Groves & Royal Gardens (`Fields`)**:
  - 6 initial permaculture beds with moisture retention, soil fertility, and 7-stage crop growth cycles.
  - Comprehensive Crop Codex: Golden Wheat, Sweet Corn, Ruby Tomato, Crisp Carrot, Aromatic Herbs, Mana Sunflower, Moon Spores, Noble Grain, Cursed Berries, and more.
  - Quick "Hydrate All" blessings and alchemical growth enrichment.

- **Ancient Mana & Mystic Circles (`Mana`)**:
  - Renewable generation via Photovoltaic arrays, Aeolian wind spires, Biogas digesters, and Hydro wheels.
  - Runic storage vessels with real-time net charge/discharge calculation.
  - 5-tier selective load shedding (Critical, Important, Normal, Comfort, Decorative) to safeguard essential kingdom life support.

- **Regenerative Menagerie (`Menagerie`)**:
  - Sanctuary livestock: Golden Sheep, Royal Cows, Island Hens, Mystic Bees, and Cloud Alpacas.
  - Care routines: Feeding, petting, happiness & hunger meters, and gathering golden fleece, sacred milk, eggs, honey, and divine fiber.
  - Adoption center to expand the kingdom's animal companionship.

- **Master's Forge & Alchemy Lab (`Forge`)**:
  - Crafting recipes combining harvests, animal offerings, and stored mana to forge Essence of Sun, Silk of the Heavens, Alchemical Salves, Elixirs, and Mystic Power Stones.

- **Trading Post & Merchant Guild (`Bazaar`)**:
  - King's Commissions fulfillment for Sol Coins and Eco Prestige.
  - Seed procurement and inventory bartering.
  - Operating business hours (8:00 AM to 6:00 PM).

- **Kingdom Chronicles (`Chronicles`)**:
  - 12 progressive Story Missions from "First Light" to "Project New Dawn".
  - Citizen sanctuary management (beds, food/water consumption, morale).
  - 15 discoverable Points of Interest across all 3 biomes.
  - Ancient encrypted terminal decryption with security cipher (`SOLARIS`).
  - Arcane Research Tech Tree and Lore Journal reader.
  - Royal Achievements showcase and celebratory epilogue.

## Running the App

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

App runs on port `3000`.
